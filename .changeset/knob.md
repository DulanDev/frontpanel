Add `FpKnob`, a rotary knob: `v-model` with `min`, `max` and `step`, turned by dragging up and down (Shift for fine moves) or with the arrow keys, PageUp, PageDown, Home and End, as an ARIA slider. `label` adds a silkscreen label that names it, `valueText` reads the value out in words, and `valueCommit` fires when a drag ends or a key moves it. The look ships as `fp-knob`, `fp-knob-mark` and `fp-knob-unit` in `@frontpanel/css`.
